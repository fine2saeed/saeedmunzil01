The Prophet ﷺ described himself as a teacher. His gatherings were a living classroom in which asking questions was welcomed, not discouraged.

## Knowledge for all

He ﷺ opened learning to men and women, rich and poor alike. One condition for freeing certain captives after Badr was that they teach the children of Madinah to read and write.

## Teaching with gentleness

He taught through gentleness and example rather than harshness. When a young man erred, the Prophet ﷺ explained with kindness rather than rebuke — and it was this manner that changed hearts.

> Indeed, I was sent as a teacher.
>
> <cite>— Sunan Ibn Majah</cite>
