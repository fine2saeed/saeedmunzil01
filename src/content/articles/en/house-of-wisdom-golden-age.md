Baghdad's House of Wisdom stands as a symbol of the golden age of Islamic civilisation. Here Greek, Persian, and Indian sciences were translated into Arabic and new research was born.

## The translation movement

In the era of Caliph al-Ma'mun, translators were reportedly rewarded with gold equal to the weight of the books they rendered. That effort raised mathematics, medicine, and astronomy to new heights.

Al-Khwarizmi's algebra, Ibn al-Haytham's optics, and Jabir ibn Hayyan's chemistry were all products of this scholarly environment.
