The central idea of Iqbal's poetry is 'Khudi' — not selfishness, but self-recognition and awareness of the abilities God has granted.

> Raise your selfhood so high that, before every decree,
> God Himself asks the servant: tell me, what is your wish?
>
> <cite>— Allama Muhammad Iqbal</cite>

For Iqbal, a person of strong Khudi does not become a slave to circumstance, but shapes their own destiny through effort and conviction.
