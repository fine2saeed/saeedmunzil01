Artificial intelligence has entered every corner of our lives. The question is no longer whether to use it, but with which values we use it.

## Intention and responsibility

In Islam, the ruling on any tool depends on its use. AI is neither good nor evil in itself — what matters is the intention behind it and the outcome it produces.

## Truth and transparency

Using AI to deceive, lie, or deny someone their rights is not permitted. Saeed Munzil's principle is 'verifiable honesty': say what is true, and what can be checked.

Technology should remain subordinate to the human being, not the other way around. This is the balance our faith teaches.
