Nurturing is not merely advice; it is action and example. Children learn what they see their elders do.

## Balancing love and boundaries

Islamic upbringing needs both affection and limits. Neither excessive strictness nor unchecked indulgence, but the moderation that marked the Prophet's ﷺ manner.

In an age of screens, social media, and rapid information, teaching children patience, adab, and a love of knowledge is the greatest responsibility of all.
